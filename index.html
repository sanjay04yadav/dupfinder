A note from the person who built this

Why this app exists — and why it's free

Last year I started making videos — teaching myself content production, shooting take after take after take. My iPhone was permanently full. Right when I wanted to film, right when something worth capturing happened: Storage Almost Full.

So I did what everyone does — I looked at cleaner apps. Every single one wanted $5–10 a month, or my data, or both. To delete files. On my own phone. We're all paying for enough subscriptions already — and if you're also paying for Tinder, honestly, that's expensive enough.

I've built software for 14 years. I grew up in India and have lived in Berlin for more than a decade, where I lead technology at a company. So I spent my evenings building the cleaner I wanted: one that runs entirely on your phone. No servers, no accounts, no data collection — which is exactly why it can be free. It costs me nothing to run, so it costs you nothing to use.

And one thing I can't ignore: back home in India, a lot of people pay for their iPhone in monthly installments — to banks, sometimes to money lenders. Someone who is still paying off their phone should not pay again every month just to manage its storage. Not in India, not in Germany, not anywhere.

DupFinder isn't magic, and it doesn't pretend to be. It does what's honestly possible on an iPhone: finds exact duplicates with mathematical proof, groups similar shots, scores blurry photos, compresses heavy videos. The rest is in your hands — and your photos never leave your phone, because there is nothing in this app that could ever send them anywhere.

S Sanjay YadavFounder · saving money for people

Questions, answered straight

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Free forever, no catch — and here's why that's believable: the app has no servers, no cloud, no compute costs. Everything runs on your phone, so there is nothing for anyone to pay for. It cost a few weeks of one engineer's work, and that's already paid.

Who built this?

Sanjay Yadav — founder, 14 years in tech, grew up in India, based in Berlin for over a decade. DupFinder is a product of TechNow GmbH, Berlin — but it began as one person’s evenings, and he still uses it on his own phone every week.

Can deleted photos be recovered?

Yes — every deletion goes through iOS's own confirmation and lands in Recently Deleted, where it's recoverable for about 30 days.

What does “similar” mean vs. “exact”?

Exact = identical file content, proven by SHA-256 hash. Similar = visually near-identical retakes and bursts found by on-device analysis — clearly separated in the app so you always know which is which.

Does anything leave my phone?

No. The app has no networking code, so scanning, analysis, and deletion all happen on your device. There is nothing to opt out of because nothing is collected.

Which iPhones does it support?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
